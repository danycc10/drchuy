## About the practice
Medicina Respiratoria is a medical practice in Piedras Negras, Coahuila, focused on the evaluation, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up of respiratory conditions. Care is individualized for patients with asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), persistent cough, shortness of breath, and sleep-related breathing disorders. Diagnostic testing and procedures are recommended based on clinical assessment and the resources available.

## Services
1. **Respiratory medicine consultations:** Medical assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up for respiratory conditions.
2. **Asthma assessment and follow-up:** Evaluation and monitoring of asthma, treatment review, and inhaler technique education.
3. **COPD assessment and follow-up:** Evaluation and follow-up of COPD symptoms, exacerbations, treatment, and inhaler technique.
4. **Spirometry and bronchodilator testing when appropriate:** Pulmonary function assessment with bronchodilator response testing when clinically indicated.
5. **Chronic cough evaluation:** Clinical assessment of persistent cough and diagnostic tests when needed.
6. **Shortness of breath evaluation:** Clinical assessment of breathlessness and appropriate follow-up testing.
7. **Sleep-related breathing studies (home respiratory polygraphy and overnight oximetry):** Clinical evaluation of snoring and suspected sleep apnea; home respiratory polygraphy and overnight oximetry as indicated.
8. **Oxygen therapy assessment and home equipment education:** Oxygenation assessment, appropriate home oxygen therapy follow-up, and equipment safety education.
9. **CPAP and BiPAP follow-up:** Assessment of positive airway pressure therapy use, comfort, and clinical response.
10. **Thoracentesis:** Diagnostic or therapeutic pleural procedure when clinically indicated.
11. **Respiratory tube placement:** Placement of tubes for respiratory procedures when appropriate.
12. **Pleural biopsy:** Pleural sampling for diagnostic analysis when medically indicated.
13. **Bronchoscopy:** Endoscopic assessment of the airways when clinically indicated.
14. **Occupational and corporate respiratory health assessment:** Respiratory occupational evaluations, spirometry for workers, and clinical respiratory health reports.

**Important:** Procedures are provided only when indicated and when appropriate facilities, equipment, and clinical safety conditions are available. Availability should be confirmed before travel.
